EVENTS & TOURS

Over the years the farm has hosted tours of the gardens and fruit trees, children's workshops, Slow Food dinners, music and sweat lodges.

We teach self-sustainability and homesteading by touching grass and getting dirty.

To arrange a tour or ask about upcoming events, get in touch.
