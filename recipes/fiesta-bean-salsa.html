FIESTA BEAN SALSA

  • 1 CAN ORGANIC BLACK BEANS - RINSED AND DRAINED
  • 1 CAN ORGANIC KIDNEY BEANS - RINSED AND DRAINED
  • 1 CAN ORGANIC PINTO, NAVY OR GARBANZO BEANS - RINSED AND DRAINED
  • 1 CAN ORGANIC SWEET CORN - DRAINED
  • 1 CUP TOMATO (ANY VARIETIES) - CHOPPED
  • 1 CUP SWEET PEPPER (ANY VARIETIES) - FINELY CHOPPED
  • 2 CUPS ONIONS - FINELY CHOPPED
  • 1/2 CUP SPRING OR GREEN ONION - FINELY CHOPPED
  • 4 CLOVES GARLIC - MINCED
  • 1/4 CUP CELERY LEAF OR SMALLAGE - FINELY CHOPPED
  • 1/4 CUP LIME OR LEMON JUICE
  • 1/4 CUP RED WINE VINEGAR
  • 1/4 OLIVE OIL
  • 3 TBS. FRESH CILANTRO - MINCED
  • 1 tsp. DRIED OREGANO
  • 1 tsp. CELERY SEED
  • 2 tsp. CAYENNE PEPPER POWDER
  • SALT AND PEPPER TO TASTE

IN A LARGE MIXING BOWL, WHISK TOGETHER: LIME JUICE, VINEGAR, OLIVE OIL, CILANTRO, CAYENNE POWDER, OREGANO, CELERY SEED AND A PINCH OF SALT AND PEPPER. ADD REMAINING INGREDIENTS, MIX WELL AND CHILL FOR AT LEAST 2 HOURS BEFORE SERVING. PLACE IN A LARGE SERVING BOWL, AND PRESENT WITH (SCOOPABLE) CORN TORTILLA CHIPS.

NOTE: ADDITIONS TO THIS SALSA MAY INCLUDE: CHOPPED HOT PEPPERS, CHOPPED AVACADO, ROASTED PEPPERS AND GARLIC, OR CHOPPED CUCUMBERS. ALSO, AS A MAIN DISH, SERVE OVER FRESH SALAD GREENS WITH FINE CHEESE AND WINE.

MAKES APPROX. 12 SIDE SERVINGS.
