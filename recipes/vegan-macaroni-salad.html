VEGAN MACARONI SALAD

  • 16oz ROTINI PASTA
  • 1 1/2 CUPS STEAMED BROCCOLI - CHOPPED
  • 1 1/2 CUPS CARROTS - SHREDDED
  • 3 1/2 CUPS ONION - CHOPPED
  • 1 2/3 CUPS CELERY - CHOPPED
  • 2/3 CUP YELLOW, RED, ORANGE BELL PEPPER - CHOPPED
  • 2 1/2 CUPS VEGANASE
  • 2 Tbs WHITE VINAGAR
  • 1 tsp FRESH GROUND PEPPER
  • 1 tsp SALT = OR TO TASTE

BOIL PASTA IN A LARGE POT. DRAIN, THEN RINSE WITH COLD WATER. ADD ALL INGREDIENTS AND STIR. REFRIDGERATE FOR AT LEAST 4 HRS. BEFORE SERVING.

MAKES APPROX. 5 POUNDS.
