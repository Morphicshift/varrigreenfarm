FRESH PEACH

  • 1 FRESH PEACH FROM BACKYARD TREE

PLUCK PEACH FROM BRANCH. PLACE JUICY PEACH INTO MOUTH. NOTICE THE JUICE AS IT ROLLS DOWN YOUR CHIN. CHEW WITH MUCH EXUBERANCE AND GLEE, WHILST CLASPING HANDS IN GESTURE OF UTTER DELIGHT. SPIT OUT PIT.

MAKES APPROXIMATELY 1 SERVING.
