CHILDREN'S WORKSHOPS

The mission of our children's workshops was to teach kids and their parents about what's actually in their food. We gave tours of the farm's gardens, and a nutritionist explained the ingredients, because what you can't pronounce probably isn't food. And we had good, clean farm fun, stuffing scarecrows and getting our hands in the soil to plant seeds.

The moment I realized teaching has a cumulative effect was when the children came back to see me at the market with vegetables they had grown themselves from the seeds I gave them. At some point in the future, with some help, we will start the workshops again.
